# Solar Power Generation Analysis

A simple and clear analysis of a *Solar Power Plant Dataset* using Python and Jupyter Notebook.  
The dataset contains *time-series data* of two solar plants with details of power generation at inverter level.  

---

## 📊 What’s Inside?
- Data from *2 solar power plants*  
- Columns: DateTime, Plant ID, Source Key, DC Power, AC Power, Daily Yield, Total Yield  
- Recorded at regular time intervals  

---

## Goals of the Project
- Understand solar power generation patterns  
- Compare *DC Power vs AC Power*  
- Track *Daily Yield* and *Total Yield*  
- Create *visualizations* for better insights  

---

## Key feature that in includes
-  Power rises in the morning, peaks at noon, and falls in the evening  
- AC Power < DC Power (due to inverter loss)  
- Energy generation varies with weather and seasons  

---

## Tools Required for this
- Python 
- Pandas & NumPy (data analysis)  
- Matplotlib & Seaborn (visualizations)  
- Jupyter Notebook (analysis & documentation)
